# PH annual compliance reminders and government export status

BIR Form 1604-C with Alphalist: January 31 of the year following the tax
year is the published standard annual deadline. BIR Form 2316: January 31
is the employer's deadline to **issue each employee a certificate**, including
MWEs and employees whose remuneration had no WHT. BIR's separate submission
requirements for copies of 2316 can have a different date and extensions;
this calendar deliberately does not conflate the two obligations.

Primary BIR references:
- https://www.bir.gov.ph/bir-forms
- https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RMC%20No.%2034-2022.pdf
- https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%2029-2024.pdf

The annual calendar reminder is a conservative planning target, NEVER
evidence of compliance; on/after the deadline it requires verification
against real BIR acknowledgement and employee delivery.

Government exports are source/reconciliation worksheets only:
SSS R-3, PhilHealth RF-1/EPRS, Pag-IBIG MCRF, BIR 1601-C and Alphalist.
The UI and HTTP response now explicitly say DRAFT-NOT-CERTIFIED.
No file is represented as a government-accepted portal upload.
Certified agency formats must be separately implemented and tested
against the current official tools with actual acknowledgement evidence.

Bank proprietary layout templates likewise remain subject to bank
validation rather than being invented or presented as certified.
