# BIR Form 2316 line mapping review

This review uses the [corrected September 2021 Form 2316](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/2316%20Sep%202021%20ENCS_Final_corrected.pdf), the [BIR 1604-C Alphalist format](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/1604C%20Alphalist%20Format%20-%20final.pdf), [RR No. 11-2018](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/local/pdf/RR%20No.%2011-2018.pdf) and [RR No. 29-2025](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%2029-2025.pdf). The line categorizer supplies a reconciliation candidate for review.

| Mapping | What the BIR sources establish | What the reviewer still needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contributions in 36 and net basic in 39 or 29 | Form 2316 assigns the employee share to 36. Alphalist Schedules 1 and 2 expressly use basic or statutory minimum wage net of the named contributions and union dues. | Confirm the employee-share statutory deductions. Supply GSIS and union-dues detail if applicable. |
| Shared benefit pool in 34 and 48, de minimis in 35 | The form caps item 34 at ₱90,000. The Alphalist names both 13th month and other benefits in its excess bucket, supporting item 48 for the combined excess. RR 11-2018 sends qualifying de minimis excess through the shared pool. | Confirm the year-specific de minimis limits and total pool use. RR 11-2018 discusses benefits paid or accrued for the exclusion, while BIR describes the certificate as reporting payments. Reconcile an unpaid accrual to issuance-period records before relying on the candidate for a certificate. |
| Exempt allowance in 37 | Item 37 is the residual exempt compensation field. Placement there is an inference once the underlying exemption is documented and the allowance has no more specific exempt field. | Record the exemption basis. An engine tax-treatment note alone does not establish it. |
| Taxable allowance in 44A | The form reserves items 40–43 for named regular allowances. RR 11-2018 distinguishes regular fixed allowances from supplementary payments. | Use the specific field where applicable. Reserve a described 44A entry for residual regular taxable allowances. The generic allowance code lacks the evidence to make this distinction automatically. |
| Ordinary night and holiday/rest-day premiums in 51A/B | Form 2316 has a supplementary residual with descriptions and no dedicated taxable line for those premiums. This supports using the residual. | The A/B order is a local convention. The reviewer confirms the supplementary classification, descriptions and source split. |
| MWE holiday and hazard in 30 and 33 | The form names MWE holiday pay separately. RR 11-2018 limits the hazard exemption to qualifying assignments and supporting evidence. | The engine's combined holiday/rest-day and calamity/hazard codes cannot establish eligibility. Hold nonzero ambiguous buckets for review. |
| Leave conversion and custom premiums | RR 29-2025 recognizes qualifying private-sector unused vacation leave within its annual day limit, with a separate rule for government leave. Tax treatment and the amount depend on the applicable rule. | Obtain leave type, sector, annual days already monetized, applicable dates and excess calculation. For a custom premium obtain the underlying pay rule and tax treatment. |
| Mixed MWE status and imported history | Items 19–21 and Part IV-B concern present-employer compensation. Item 22 separately identifies applicable previous-employer taxable compensation. BIR ties MWE treatment to statutory wages and specified pay. The item 29 label also mentions exempt compensation at or below ₱250,000. | A year-level MWE flag cannot distinguish mixed periods or resolve item 29 for a low-income ordinary employee. Require period-level wage, location, eligibility and payment records. Establish whether a prior provider served the same legal employer, or whether a different employer issued a prior 2316. Keep imported records without a usable split under review. |

The next integration step is a review-only preview with explicit payroll evidence for the unresolved rows. Use paid or released source records, distinguish legal employers and tax periods, and compare the detail against both the year-end summary and the Alphalist totals. This source review does not authorize issuance or submission.
